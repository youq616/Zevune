**D1 is closed in the re-reviewed document.** It now requires bounded immutable `progress-0001.json` and subsequent sequence files, with an operation/payment/actual-commit-count record persisted before each potentially blocking operation and a matching completed record carrying measured duration afterward. A fixed operation allowlist, bounds, matching starts/completions, one in-flight operation and explicit incomplete-state semantics are specified. The supervisor continuously collects this sequence, reads again after Go exits, and retains completed records plus the last unfinished operation on failure. Missing sequence entries, malformed fields and progress-save failures fail acceptance. The successful final timing result must agree with the completed progress records. ## Reviewed design conclusions

1. **Fixed genuine load.** Thirty-two normal one-payment blocks and a thirty-third real payment after restoration are bounded, explicit and separately counted from the older empty-block growth experiment. The explicit `--active-resource-v1` scenario mode preserves previous modes and does not add limit overrides, direct state changes, prebuilt journals, mock proof acceptance or a production interface.
2. **Exact accounting.** With A/B starting at 50000, each payment amount/fee both 1000, and A sending odd-index payments, the table is consistent: height 32 yields A/B 34000/34000, fees 32000, 66 commitments and 64 nullifiers; height 33 yields 32000/35000, fees 33000, 68 commitments and 66 nullifiers. Exactly two decoded Orchard actions per payment must be checked against actual generated bytes. The document correctly explains that padded action/nullifier counts are not real-input counts.
3. **Wallet records.** Current create-plus-genesis-sync produces two saved records per wallet. Each new committed height adds one saved checkpoint for both; the sender adds one pending-payment save. Thus 50/50 at height 32, 51/50 with payment 33 pending, and 52/51 after its commit/sync are consistent with the present WalletStore path. Restoring the same checkpoint/outbox must preserve record counts. The implementation must validate these against storage_status and actual file metadata; the table alone is not evidence.
4. **Complete recovery order.** The first worker is sampled and closed; a new worker performs complete replay and must recover the same Summary and capacity. The scenario opens its independent same-source ledger and restores the receipt-bound encrypted wallets before building payment 33. It then separately reopens the pending backup and proves exact outbox bytes, submits that same transaction, and checks availability and cleared pending state. This distinguishes newly constructing a payment after recovery from merely recovering prebuilt bytes.
5. **Non-mutation and real authorization.** The proposed boundary tests check selection, preview/finalization consistency, pre-expiry duplicate rejection and unchanged Summary/capacity/journal bytes. A wrong commit tag must preserve the already-finalized real payment 33 so its genuine tag still commits. Full frame accounting is explicitly supplementary to genuine replay/authorization.
6. **Nine-event coverage.** The event sequence and heights 0/8/16/24/32/32/32/32/33 are coherent. Worker generation 1 is observed through payment_32; generation 2 is observed from worker_reopened_32 through complete_33; scenario generation 1 is observed throughout. These events sample each worker before its closure and the scenario after the final relevant workload. No metric is imported across PID reuse. Final cleanup remains outside the stated observed workload window.
7. **Memory scope.** The per-process 1073741824-byte gate measures OS-reported resident/working-set lifecycle high-water, with current snapshots also retained. It is neither a hard allocation cap nor private-commit, heap-only, phase-local or whole-system memory. It separates worker/scenario from the Go/Python supervisors and states that scenario combines two wallets, a ledger, prover, verifier/cache, history and Argon2. Process parent and creation-identity checks, unavailable metrics and failed persistence are specified as fail-closed conditions. No observed-budget success is claimed before native execution.
8. **Timing scope.** Fixed 60-second worker startup/request, 90-second scenario response, 15-second resource handshake, 20-minute Go total and at most 30 seconds extra supervisor cleanup are explicit. The startup target is correctly described as stricter than the old growth scenario's five minutes. Operation timings stop after response validation and exclude later memory handshakes; total duration includes broader supervision/checking. Failure evidence is now preserved by the corrected progress protocol.
9. **Cache and temperature limitations.** Complete state-rule replay is not relabeled cold proof verification when the existing 64-entry verifier cache can be used. New worker and PoolStore open get new verifiers; same-process scenario recovery retains prover/allocator and may use a warm OS file cache. The design does not claim fresh-process wallet recovery, cold-disk timing, production TPS, privacy-network latency or cross-platform performance ratios.
10. **Scope stays limited.** This baseline does not cover authorization-cache eviction, the 64-anchor boundary, 65536 commitments, 256 wallet saves, activity-segment rotation/full capacity, archives/snapshots/pruning, real power loss/disk full, long multi-machine operation or specialist external security review. P2 remains in progress.

## Primary metric references

- Linux kernel /proc: https://docs.kernel.org/filesystems/proc.html . VmHWM is resident high-water; VmRSS is resident accounting and can be approximate.
- Microsoft PROCESS_MEMORY_COUNTERS: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/psapi/ns-psapi-process_memory_counters . WorkingSetSize and PeakWorkingSetSize are current/peak working sets.
- Microsoft PROCESS_MEMORY_COUNTERS_EX: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/psapi/ns-psapi-process_memory_counters_ex . PrivateUsage/PagefileUsage represent private commit and are not substitute RSS fields.
